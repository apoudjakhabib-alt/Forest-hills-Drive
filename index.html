Getting Started

Soccer is one of the most popular sports worldwide. It's easy to learn and fun to play.

Here are a few basics every beginner should know before stepping onto the field.

  • Wear comfortable cleats and shin guards.
  • Practice passing and dribbling daily.
  • Stay hydrated and warm up before games.
